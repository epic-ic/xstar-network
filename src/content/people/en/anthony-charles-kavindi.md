Anthony Charles Kavindi is an emerging researcher in vector-borne disease modelling and a PhD candidate based in Tanzania at Kilimanjaro Christian Medical University College (KCMC), within the School of Public Health, Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics. He has an academic background in Mathematics and Statistics and holds a Master’s degree in Epidemiology and Biostatistics.

His current doctoral research focuses on modelling vector-borne disease transmission and spatial risk patterns in Northern Tanzania in the context of climate and environmental change. The project is supervised by Dr Debora Kajeguka, co-Principal Investigator of the xSTAR Project at KCMC University, Professor Sia Msuya (KCMC University) and co-supervised by Professor Ilaria Dorigatti at Imperial College London.
